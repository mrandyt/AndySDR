FT8 and FT4 are decoded in USB mode from the 12 kHz audio, once per 15 s (FT4: 7.5 s) UTC time slot, so the PC clock must be right. "Sync" is the decoder's candidate score, not a calibrated SNR. Tune a band-plan dial frequency (e.g. 14.074 MHz) and AndySDR starts the decoder by itself when something is on the air.

        Switch a satellite on and AndySDR works out every pass over your location, tunes in and starts the decoder by itself.

The receiver cannot measure the current it supplies. This watches the noise floor instead: a powered LNA or active antenna lifts it, and a sudden drop means the power has gone. For the actual current use a USB power meter or a DC meter on the feed.
