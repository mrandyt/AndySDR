RF gainhow much the dongle amplifies – you can change it while it is working
          –

        
        
Turn it up until the background noise (the grass on the trace) just starts to rise, but stop before the waterfall turns bright all over or lots of false spikes appear – that means it is too high. There is no single right number - it depends on your antenna and dongle, so try a few steps over a pass and keep the one that gives the best SNR.

      

      

        
        
Meteor sends a signal that spins as it travels. A straight antenna – a V-dipole, a discone, a whip –
          loses it for a moment each time the spin turns away, which is what leaves the black bands across a picture.
          Two aerials pointing different ways rarely lose it at the same moment. With this on, the main radio records the
          pass as well, and afterwards the frames each receiver heard are put together into one fuller set of pictures.
          The main radio stops scanning for the few minutes the satellite is overhead.

    🛰 Satellite auto-capture
        Switch a satellite on and AndySDR works out every pass over your location, tunes in and starts the decoder by itself.
    
      
    📍 AndySDR needs your position to work out passes.
    
      
    ℹ Only satellites that rise above your horizon are listed. NOAA 15, 18 and 19 (137 MHz APT) were retired in 2025. From the UK you can receive Meteor-M N2-3 / N2-4 (137 MHz LRPT, simple antenna + LNA), Metop-B / Metop-C (1701.3 MHz AHRPT, dish + L-band LNA) and ISS SSTV during events.

      Antenna map – where in the sky is your signal strong or weak?
      
    
        
        
        
      
    
      
    
        
        
    
          
    Straight up is the centre, the horizon is the outer ring and north is at the top. Each dot is a moment in a pass, coloured by how strong the signal was for that distance – green is strong, red is weak. A patch of red on one side means the antenna is poor in that direction: try turning or tilting it, then check the map again after a few passes.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    Tips: Met Office Northwood fax charts are sent throughout the day – look up the current broadcast schedule and add a capture for the charts you want. ISS SSTV events are announced by ARISS a week or two ahead. Meteor-M passes over the UK happen several times a day; a satellite-pass app gives the times. Pictures appear in WEATHER IMAGES.
